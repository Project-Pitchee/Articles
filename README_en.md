# Pitchee Articles

Each assessment measures your voice with three scores:

1. **Timbre score**: how feminine the overall voice sounds.
2. **Naturalness**: how closely the recording resembles normal speech.
3. **Average pitch**: the average pitch of voiced portions in the recording, measured in Hz.

These scores produce one overall result. The current pass line is 60. A score below 60 means the recording has not met the standard for this assessment. A score of 60 or above means the voice has passed the initial threshold.

## Results

| Your result | Outcome |
| --- | --- |
| Pitch, naturalness, and timbre score are all high | A bonus may apply, with a maximum near or at 100 |
| Pitch is high enough and naturalness is below 50 | Maximum 30 |
| Pitch is at or below 165 Hz and naturalness is at least 50 | Maximum 59 |
| Pitch is at or below 165 Hz and naturalness is below 50 | Maximum 20 |
| Pitch is high enough, naturalness is at least 50, and timbre score is below 50 | Maximum 59 |
| None of the above applies and the overall score is below 60 | All three individual scores passed, but the overall result did not |
| None of the above applies and the overall score is at least 60 | Initial pass |
